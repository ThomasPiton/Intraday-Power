What this is

A static terminal refreshed every 15 minutes by GitHub Actions. It tracks (1) how intraday renewable forecasts move against day-ahead in five bidding zones, (2) two paper-trading bots that take a 1 MW position in Belgium between the day-ahead price and the imbalance price, and (3) what a 1 MW / 2 MWh battery could have earned on day-ahead prices in each zone. Every number comes from the public series below; nothing is simulated or interpolated beyond the resampling stated here. If a series is missing, the page says no data.

Forecast revisions

revision_MW = (solar + wind_on + wind_off)intraday − (solar + wind_on + wind_off)day-ahead per zone and QH; a component only counts where both its forecasts exist. revision_pct = revision_MW / day-ahead total (blank when the DA total is below 50 MW). Alerts fire when |revision| exceeds the zone's 95th percentile since 2024 (…).

Bots: trade and P&L

Each bot holds p ∈ {−1, 0, +1} MW on quarter-hour Q, bought/sold at the Belgian day-ahead price and settled at the Elia imbalance price:

pnl_EUR = p × (imbalance_price_Q − DA_BE_Q) × 0.25 h − 0.5 EUR/MWh × |p| × 0.25 h

This is the P&L of a deliberately open position, i.e. a proxy for the value of an intraday view. It is paper trading: no market impact, no intraday bid-ask, no balancing-responsibility penalties beyond the single imbalance price.

Read the backtest with care. Most of the edge comes from the persistence of the Belgian imbalance spread: the realised (imbalance − DA) of Q−3 is the ML bot's top feature, and a rule on the 30-minute-old SI alone has a ~64% hit ratio. The spread was extreme after MARI go-live (mean |imbalance − DA| ≈ 156 €/MWh in summer 2024). That signal is real and passes the lookahead tests, but it is not a capturable P&L at scale: a BRP must stay balanced over its perimeter, Elia's pricing (α component) penalises large deviations, and any volume taking this view moves the imbalance itself. The live record is the measure that matters.

Anti-lookahead protocol

  • The decision for Q is taken as of d(Q) = Q − 20 min (minute 10 of Q−2).
  • Allowed: the DA price of Q's delivery day; quarter-hour real-time data (SI, imbalance price, minute-price last/mean/slope) up to Q−3, which ended 30 min before Q and is published 1–7 min after its end (measured); Elia SI-forecast vintages with publication time ≤ d(Q) (they forecast Q−1); calendar.
  • This is stricter than the usual “Q−2”: Q−2 is not reliably published at a time a 15-min GitHub cron can act on, and backtest and live must see the same information.
  • Live: a run at time t decides only the QHs with Q − 17 min ≤ t ≤ Q − 2 min, appends {bot, qh_start_utc, decided_at_utc, position, features_hash, model} to an append-only CSV journal and never edits it. A decision with decided_at ≥ qh_start is rejected by code. If an input is not yet published, the QH is skipped (no trade) rather than decided on incomplete data. Settlement rows are appended once Elia publishes the price.
  • Backtest: the same build_features() and decide() code on the stored history. A unit test scrambles every value outside the information set (all data at or after Q, every later SI-forecast vintage, later DA days) and checks that features and both bots' decisions are unchanged; a second test checks that touching data inside the set does change them.
  • Elia's next-QH SI forecast is used by both bots because its historical vintages carry publication timestamps (ods147). Renewable forecast revisions are not bot inputs: Energy-Charts and Elia keep only the final intraday forecast without issue time, so a backtest on them could leak information.

Rule bot

Signal = Elia's latest next-QH SI forecast available at d(Q) (else the last published QH SI). Elia convention: SI < 0 = system short → imbalance price tends to exceed DA. If signal < −T → +1 MW; if > +T → −1 MW; else flat. T = … MW was chosen once on the first 3 months (22 May – 22 Aug 2024, max P&L over 0–500 MW) and frozen in code; those 3 months are excluded from the reported backtest.

ML bot

LightGBM classifier of sign(imbalance − DA). Features: lagged SI (Q−3, Q−4, mean Q−3…Q−6), lagged realised spread imbalance−DA, minute imbalance price of Q−3 (last and mean vs DA, OLS slope), Elia SI forecast and its short/long probabilities as of d(Q), DA price, DA minus its daily mean, DA change vs previous QH, hour, quarter, weekday, Belgian holiday. Walk-forward: first model after 3 months, retrained every Monday 00:00 UTC on all outcomes published before the cut-off, used until the next cut-off. Trade if P(imb > DA) > 0.6 (+1) or < 0.4 (−1). Every model is saved in data/models/.

Statistics

Total P&L; P&L per trade (trades = QHs with p ≠ 0); hit ratio = share of trades with P&L > 0; Sharpe = mean/std of daily P&L (Europe/Paris days) × √365; max drawdown on cumulative daily P&L; exposure = trades / decided QHs. Backtest and live are reported separately; the live start is the vertical marker. Elia plans a new imbalance-price formula from 24 Nov 2026 (subject to CREG approval), which is also marked: results across it are not like-for-like.

Battery League

1 MW / 2 MWh, round-trip efficiency 88% (√0.88 on each leg), at most 2 full cycles per day (≤ 4 MWh discharged from the cells), SoC 1 MWh at start and end of each local day. LP per zone and day on the day's quarter-hour DA prices, solved with HiGHS (scipy.optimize.linprog):

max Σ p_t (d_t − c_t) Δt   s.t.  0 ≤ c_t, d_t ≤ 1 MW
soc_t = 1 + Δt Σ_{k≤t} (η c_k − d_k/η),  0 ≤ soc_t ≤ 2,  soc_T = 1,  Δt Σ d_t/η ≤ 4

This is a theoretical maximum with perfect foresight on DA; it ignores intraday and balancing revenues, degradation, fees and forecast error. On negative-price days the LP may charge and discharge in the same QH to burn energy through losses, which is a real (if small) value of flexibility.

Limitations

  • No EPEX continuous intraday prices (not freely available): revisions and imbalance prices are used as proxies for intraday value.
  • Paper trading only; 1 MW is assumed to be a price-taker.
  • Hourly history is forward-filled to quarter-hours; QH shape before 15-min products is not observed.
  • Live coverage depends on GitHub's cron punctuality: QHs without a run in their decision window are not traded (coverage is shown as decided QHs).
  • Imbalance prices are first-publication (“not validated”) values when settled live; Elia may revise them later.
